Number Theory

Number theory studies the structure and properties of integers. From the simplest counting to the profound mysteries of prime distribution, it reveals the delicate tension between "finite" and "infinite".

First Principle: Integers, Addition, Multiplication

Starting from the simplest object (1) and operation (addition), continuously generating and infinitely expanding. This is the First Principle's "one-to-many".

Modular Arithmetic

Infinitely many integers, after dividing by a fixed p, can only leave finitely many possible remainders: 0, 1, 2, ..., p-1. This is forcing infinite into finite from outside.

Important Theorem

Fermat's Little Theorem

For any a not divisible by p: a^(p-1) ≡ 1 (mod p)
