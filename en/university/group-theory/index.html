Group Theory

Group theory studies the pure form of closed operation systems. It emerges from a shift in perspective: instead of calculating on objects, we now study "the way of calculating" as a new object itself.

The Four Axioms

Closure

Operation result never leaves the set

Associativity

$(ab)c = a(bc)$

Identity

$e \cdot a = a$

Inverse

$a^-1 \cdot a = e$

Important Theorems

Lagrange's Theorem

The order of a subgroup divides the order of the group.

Sylow Theorems

Precise characterization of subgroups of prime power order.

Galois Correspondence

Field extensions correspond precisely to subgroups of the Galois group.
