Topology

Topology studies the continuity and neighborhood relations of spaces. It doesn't care about exact distances or angles, but about the more essential structure of "which points are close together".

Core Concept: Properties Invariant Under Continuous Deformation

Homeomorphism - two spaces that can be continuously stretched, bent but not torn or glued together are considered "the same" in topology. A coffee cup and a donut are homeomorphic.

Open Set Axioms

Empty Set and Whole Space

∅ and X are both open sets

Arbitrary Union

Arbitrary union of open sets is open

Finite Intersection

Finite intersection of open sets is open

Important Theorems

Brouwer Fixed Point Theorem

Every continuous map from a disk to itself has a fixed point. This is a direct manifestation of the Second Principle - you cannot escape from inside.

Jordan Curve Theorem

Every simple closed curve in the plane divides the plane into interior and exterior regions.
