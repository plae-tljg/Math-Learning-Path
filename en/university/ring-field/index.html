Ring & Field

Rings and fields are algebraic structures with multiplication added on top of groups. From integers to polynomials, from cryptography to geometry, rings and fields provide a richer algebraic language.

Ring Axioms

Additive Abelian Group

(R, +) is an abelian group

Multiplicative Semigroup

(R, ·) satisfies associativity

Distributivity

a(b + c) = ab + ac

Field Conditions

A field requires multiplication to form an abelian group (excluding zero). This means all elements except 0 are invertible. Typical fields: Q, R, C, and finite fields F_p.

Important Theorems

Unique Factorization Domain

Every nonzero non-unit element can be uniquely factored into irreducible elements. The integer ring Z is a typical unique factorization domain.

Ideals and Quotient Rings

Ideals are structures in rings similar to normal subgroups, used to construct quotient rings.
