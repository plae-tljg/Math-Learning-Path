Category Theory

Category theory is the "meta-language" of mathematics, studying the relationships between morphisms to provide a unified abstract framework for different areas of mathematics.

Why Learn Category Theory?

"Category theory is the mathematics that mathematicians invented to talk about mathematics."

Core Concepts

Objects

The basic elements of a category - can be sets, groups, topological spaces, or any mathematical object.

Morphisms

Arrows between objects, representing structure-preserving mappings.

Composition

Composition of morphisms, $g \circ f$ means first apply $f$, then apply $g$.

Identity Morphism

The identity arrow of each object, leaving the object unchanged.

Important Theorem

Yoneda Lemma

One of the most fundamental results in category theory, stating that an object is completely determined by its morphisms to other objects.

Nat(Hom(A,-), F) ≅ F(A)
